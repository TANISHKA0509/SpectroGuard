WHY THIS MATTERS — THE BRIEFING
Fraud

Deepfake video calls are now a business risk

Face-swapped voices and faces are being used to bypass identity checks and authorize fake transfers. A single convincing call is enough.

Trust

Human detection is no better than a coin flip

Studies show people correctly judge real vs. synthetic media only around 50–60% of the time — machines can do much better.

Cost

Scams are getting cheaper to produce

Cloning a voice or face now needs seconds of footage and free tools, dropping the barrier for attackers to near zero.

• Every video call could feature a face that isn’t there. • Detectors must keep up with generators. • A deepfake can be created from seconds of footage. • Trust is the target — verification is the defense. • SpectroGuard: frame-by-frame forensic check.

Want to check a video — is it real or fake?

Upload a clip and SpectroGuard samples its frames, runs them through a pre-trained model, and returns a REAL / FAKE verdict with a confidence score.
